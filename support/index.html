Contact

Email support@arcpace.app, or use Send feedback in the app's Settings. Replies come by email.

Before you write

  • No forecast: check that ArcPace can use your location (Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > ArcPace).
  • No past runs: check that ArcPace can read workouts in the Health app (Health > your profile > Apps > ArcPace).
  • Widget out of date: open ArcPace once and it refreshes.
Questions: support@arcpace.app
