Philadelphia Marathon weather: 10 years of race days

Updated 2026-11-11 · General guidance, not medical advice

Short answer: the Philadelphia Marathon is a cold, dry race that is often windy. In the last 10, it was 33–50°F at the 7 AM start with a dew point of 45°F or lower every year, so heat is rarely the problem. Wind is: six of the 10 had 10+ mph by 11 AM, and in 2016 and 2017 it passed 20 mph.

The 2026 Philadelphia Marathon is Sunday, November 22. Runners start at 7:00 AM on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway near the Philadelphia Museum of Art, run through Center City, University City and Fairmount Park along the Schuylkill River, and finish at the Art Museum steps.

What those days did to pace #

Four years through the ArcPace calculator at a 10:00/mi pace. Pace cost is the seconds per mile the weather adds against an ordinary mild day; negative means the weather helps.

  • 2023, cold, calm and sunny: 93 (Ideal) at 7 AM and 99 (Ideal) at 11 AM. Pace cost −3 and −7 s/mi: the weather helped.
  • 2019, raw and wet: 79 (Good) at 7 AM and 77 (Good) at 11 AM. Pace cost −2 and +4 s/mi; rain and wind held the scores down.
  • 2022, freezing and windy: 83 (Good) at 7 AM and 69 (Good) at 11 AM. Pace cost +6 and +9 s/mi, from the wind.
  • 2016, the windiest: 75 (Good) at 7 AM and 57 (Moderate) at 11 AM, with 23 mph wind. Pace cost +5 and +6 s/mi.

None of these years had a heat flag above White. In Philadelphia the wind decides a hard year, and the cold decides what you wear.

Reading this year's forecast #

  • Look at two hours: 7 AM and the hour you expect to finish. The last 10 races went from 1°F cooler to 16°F warmer between 7 and 11 AM.
  • Wind first. 15+ mph means tucking into a group on the exposed river stretches and running by effort into the wind.
  • Rain: light rain at 40°F is colder than it reads. A cap and a throwaway poncho for the start help.
  • Dew point: 45°F or lower in every recent race, so humidity is rarely a factor here.

More on this in marathon race-day weather: how to read the forecast and running in rain and wind.

What to wear, by start temperature #

  • 30s: long sleeve with a light vest, tights or shorts if you run warm, light gloves and something over the ears. In wind, a wind jacket. Old layers for the corral, dropped before the start.
  • 40s: tee with arm warmers or a light long sleeve, shorts, cheap gloves. A throwaway layer for the wait.
  • 50s: tee and shorts.

Questions #

What is the usual weather for the Philadelphia Marathon?

Cold and dry at the start, often windy later. In the last 10 races, 7 AM near the Art Museum was 33–50°F with a dew point of 45°F or lower every year. By 11 AM the wind was 10 mph or more in six of them.

What was the toughest recent Philadelphia Marathon weather?

Wind, not heat. In 2016 the wind reached 23 mph by 11 AM under cloud; ArcPace scores that hour 57 (Moderate), costing a 10:00/mi runner about 6 seconds per mile. 2022 was the coldest: 33°F at the start and 34°F with 17 mph wind at 11 AM.

When is the Philadelphia Marathon forecast reliable?

The National Weather Service hourly forecast reaches race day about a week out. Plan on it from two or three days before, and check again the night before.
